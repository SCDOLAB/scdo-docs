# Now hiring: GPU compute contributors

SCDO Laboratory is a blockchain technology company. We are looking for people who want to share their GPU with the SCDO network.

**Position:** GPU compute contributor

**Location:** Remote. No interview, no sign-up, no wallet needed to start.

**Requirements**

* A Windows PC
* An NVIDIA GPU with 4 GB or more of VRAM

**How to apply**

1. Download the SCDO GPU Share worker (about 9 MB) from the [download page](https://apeccapital.org/gpu-share/?ref=gitbook-recruit).
2. Unzip it and double-click the worker.
3. It creates your address and connects to the SCDO public pool on its own. Close the window whenever you want it to stop.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The first run creates a wallet key at `%APPDATA%\SCDO\gpu-share\worker.key`. Back it up. Your address depends on it.
{% endhint %}

The worker also supports Folding@home medical research through team **SCDO Laboratory** (team number 1068523).

Step-by-step guide: [Install the worker](install.md). Questions: [FAQ](faq.md).
