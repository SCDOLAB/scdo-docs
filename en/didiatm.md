# didiATM

**In development — coming soon.** didiATM is not live yet.

didiATM is being built by 9Y9 PTY LTD, trading as SCDO Laboratory, as a peer-to-peer cash and crypto exchange network: customers post orders, nearby merchants take them, and the crypto side of each order is held in an escrow contract on SCDO Shard0 EVM until the order completes.

Details will be published here when it launches.
